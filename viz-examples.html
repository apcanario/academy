Figure kinds

TimelineCompareTreeFlowSitemapObject mapDecision treeMatrixState diagramSwimlanesWireflow

Every kind of figure a unit can declare, drawn by the build from a fenced viz block. The four first kinds came with A8; the seven after them were added for the Information Architecture course (V1, 29 October 2026). Each example uses Perqo, the course's fictional benefits platform: its names and numbers are invented for this page. Under each figure, the block that draws it.

Timeline · timeline

Decision tree · decision

Fields: root {q, tone, branches [{label, to}]} or {outcome, tone}; every kind also takes title, caption and tone (1 to 6).
