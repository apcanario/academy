Figure kinds

TimelineCompareTreeFlowSitemapObject mapDecision treeMatrixState diagramSwimlanesWireflowLine chartBarsScatterRangeMatrix, numeric

Every kind of figure a unit can declare, drawn by the build from a fenced viz block. The four first kinds came with A8; the seven after them were added for the Information Architecture course (V1, 29 October 2026); the last four, and the matrix's numeric mode, are for data (P44, 29 October 2026). On a phone a figure keeps its natural size and scrolls sideways inside its frame. Each example uses Perqo, the course's fictional benefits platform: its names and numbers are invented for this page. Under each figure, the block that draws it.
