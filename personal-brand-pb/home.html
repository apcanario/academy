COURSE PB · Personal Brand (public) · IN PROGRESS

Building a Personal Brand on Instagram and YouTube

For an adult with a real skill, a full-time job and 3 to 10 hours a week: what to expect, how to position the account, how to build a publishing system and make the pieces with a phone, how growth works on YouTube and on Instagram, what the rules are, what pays, and how to read the numbers each quarter. Worked throughout on one fictional creator, Mara Venn, and shipped each time as a template. Ten core units in four parts and two deep dives.

What you will be able to do

By the end of the course you can state in one sentence who your account is for and what it gives them; have run a publishing system for twelve weeks inside an hours budget you set yourself; and read your Instagram and YouTube numbers against sourced benchmarks each quarter to decide, with evidence, whether to continue, change format or stop.

  • Outcome 1 — a positioning a stranger can repeat, live on both profiles (PB2, PB3; DD-PB1 sets it beside six documented creators).
  • Outcome 2 — a publishing system held for twelve weeks inside your own hours budget (PB4, PB5, PB8).
  • Outcome 3 — a quarterly decision to continue, change format or stop, made on your numbers against sourced benchmarks (PB1, PB6, PB7, PB9, PB10; DD-PB2).

Every unit is sourced to peer-reviewed work, platform-official statements or large-sample data, labels anecdote and vendor data as such, says where no source exists, and ends with a deliverable built in full for a fictional creator and then as a template.

    Frame

    A guide that says what the evidence supports, and where there is none

    Most advice on growing an account is one person's survivor story or a vendor's sales page. This course keeps the questions a beginner asks (what should I expect, what do I post, how does the algorithm work, when does it pay) and answers each only as far as a source allows: platform documentation, large datasets with their sample and date, peer-reviewed studies, and creator cases with verifiable numbers. Where no source meets that standard the unit says so. It is written for someone who has a job and a few hours a week, on a phone, on two platforms: Instagram for reach and YouTube for depth.

    Pick

    Ten steps in reading order, one fictional creator, every deliverable twice.

    • Steps in order: decide, build, grow, protect, earn, review.
    • Mara's worked case keeps deliverables built, never blank forms.
    • A template after each lets the reader do their own.

    Override if: after PB4 Mara's case cannot carry a reader in a very different niche; then a second, contrasting case is added from PB5 on.

    • Reader: an adult with a real skill, a full-time job, under 1,000 followers, a phone and 3 to 10 hours a week. Every brief says so.
    • Example: Mara Venn, fictional: an accountant who restores mid-century furniture at weekends. Every fact about her is invented by the unit that needs it, labelled, and kept in a register.
    • Neutral ground: no country's tax figures, no single currency; law is a list of questions with the primary source to open for the EU, the UK and the US.
    • Where the evidence pushes back: most published growth figures are vendor data and correlational, and creator histories are a sample of survivors. The course labels both and keeps them out of any deliverable they cannot support.
