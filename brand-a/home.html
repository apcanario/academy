What you will be able to do

By the end of the course the reader can state, in one sentence, who his audience is and what they pay for; run a publishing system that holds a bi-weekly cadence inside 5–7 h/week for twelve months; and read his own channel metrics against sourced benchmarks to decide, with evidence, whether to continue, change format or stop.

  • Outcome 1 — a positioning a stranger can repeat, tested against five sourced comparables (A1, DD-A1).
  • Outcome 2 — a publishing system that holds bi-weekly for twelve months inside the hour budget (A3, A5, A8).
  • Outcome 3 — a metrics sheet and benchmark table he reads quarterly to decide continue / change format / stop (A6, A9, A7, DD-A2).

Not content for its own sake: every unit is sourced to peer-reviewed, platform-official or large-sample data, labels anecdote and vendor data as such, and ends with a shipped deliverable.

Frame

The brand is the method, and the method is the record

Working name to be decided. "I get consumed by something, build a syllabus, go on the journey, and record it so I don't forget it." Karate was season one. The half marathon, the weight loss and the history course are the next journeys; building with AI is the fourth. Plurality is the register, not the hook: each piece leads with one journey and the breadth shows across pieces. ADHD is public under his name (decided 27 Oct 2026); everything else gets a pre-mortem before first disclosure. Failures stay in the record.

Pick

Journeys, recorded. Shōmen is one path off it, not the engine. Leaving corporate is not on the table for 24 months; this is a build alongside Infios, measured quarterly.

  • Generalist brands die on variety and live on a repeatable format.
  • The ADHD-creator space sells relatability; this has rigour.
  • Every existing output already fits: vlog, syllabi, journal, pcblueprint.

Override if: after six pieces in the format nobody can describe the channel back to him in one sentence. Then the format is wrong, not the breadth.

  • Cost: 5–7 h/week; €0–300 in gear; an editor only after the cadence has held for six months.
  • Horizon: 18–36 months to 1,000 YouTube subscribers at a bi-weekly cadence; a brand that moves Shōmen revenue is a 24-month question at best.
  • Where the research pushes back: the data says season one is one journey in one format, and karate is the thin niche where a 10–20K channel is visible. History and AI are a year-two layer by the format-drift evidence. His call.
